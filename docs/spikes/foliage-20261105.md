# Host-authoritative foliage breaks

The owner reports that a shrub broken by the host's car stays intact visually on the guest. Foliage can be a `BP_Crash_Complex_Foliage_Base_C` actor or a mesh instance inside an `InstancedStaticMeshComponent`; matching only actors missed the second representation.

`foliage.lua` records a host foliage actor's mesh and last intact position once per break. Optional `foliage` negotiation enables reliable, world-scoped break notifications. The guest breaks a matching local foliage actor or removes the matching instance of the same mesh within 100 uu. Instance searches run in bounded slices (64 instance queries / 2 ms per tick), and missing streamed-out targets retry for up to five minutes. No guest chassis simulation is enabled. Original positions are retained so flying broken debris cannot produce further break locations. Records replay after a guest finishes admission.

Both test-pair executable hashes match the supported Steam executable. Test pair `D:/PTLanes/vehicle-proxy`, port 7833; real install/saves unchanged. On 2026-11-05 the existing test junctions were corrected from the removed vehicle-proxy worktree to the current `C:/Dev/pacific-together` checkout.

Executed positive: `local/test-results/20261105-085201-vehicle-proxy-foliage`, 20/20. The two real game processes load the forest; an actual host shrub is placed at an isolated test position, and an instance of its exact mesh is created on the guest. Calling the shrub's own `ComplexBreak` removes that client instance. This tests the actor-to-instance replication path; it is not a human drive through every foliage species.

Negative control `20261105-085445-vehicle-proxy-foliage-nofoliage`: 18/20, exactly the host/client instance-removal checks failed, without a crash or Lua error. Tunnel 150 ms / 2% loss: `20261105-085629-vehicle-proxy-tunnel-foliage`, 26/26; gameplay carrier and lag checks passed on both sides.

Final weak-identity component guards: `20261105-085755-vehicle-proxy-foliage`, 20/20, no crash or PT error. The bundled Lua interpreter also compiled the shipping module and registration file.

Limits: the match needs the same mesh asset and nearby world position. Worlds with different procedural foliage layouts, meshes that change at the breaking edge, extended streaming gaps, and long drives still require in-game validation. Existing vehicle smoothness limitations are separate from this visual destruction fix.

## Follow-up after the owner's failed drive

The previous synthetic pass did not establish that an actual car impact was mirrored. Live logs were preserved under
`local/live-report-20261105-0933/`; the running preview was already 0.8.9, so this report cannot be dismissed as an old version.

Observations: garage foliage switches between placed actors and mesh instances. A placed actor disappearing after the car
moves is not, by itself, evidence of a break. Swapped actors carry `ST_InstanceSwappableData`: the original mesh and instance
transform survive after the visible actor moves or is removed. Live `PostBreakLogic` hooks were observed on actual driving
breaks at (-2434.04,-1708.98,31.04), (-2232.79,-615.92,25.30), and (-2149.57,-535.65,34.58).

The implementation now publishes from that event, uses the original instance transform on both sides, and skips transient
preview actors. The guest checks actors before enumerating mesh instances, avoiding that scan when an actor matches.
Applied actor/instance removals log their positions for subsequent hand-play reports.

The strengthened `foliage` scenario moves the actor 500 uu away from its saved source transform and destroys it immediately
after the break, in the same callback. It must remove the client instance at the original position. Executed:
`local/test-results/20261105-094332-vehicle-proxy-foliage`, 20/20. This covers the missed polling edge and original position;
it still does not represent every species or a complete human drive.

## Host-timed car snapshots

Optional `car-motion` negotiation adds a 20 Hz newest-wins motion stream containing the host's sampling time, position,
rotation and velocity. The guest interpolates against those times rather than estimating travel time from packet arrival
and distance. Existing framing and author protocol 7 are unchanged. Legacy peers keep the old follower. The guest chassis
and controller stay kinematic; this does not re-enable the unstable physical proxy.

`PT_VARIANT=motion` can now run `carsync` without `-Fixture`, opening the clean garage map. The save-based attempt
`20261105-105408-vehicle-proxy-carsync` failed to load because the game reported missing DLC entitlement while Steam was
not running; no ownership check was bypassed. An intermediate attempt `20261105-105531-vehicle-proxy-carsync` still tried
the default fixture slot because an empty environment variable was truthy in Lua; the test condition was corrected.

Executed clean-map motion run: `20261105-105753-vehicle-proxy-carsync`, 38/38. Guest motion: 759 samples, maximum sampled
step 52.7 uu, zero stalls, maximum sampling interval 135 ms. Seat, orientation, settled position and camera checks passed.
Actual host foliage breaks were observed during this drive; a subsequent guest probe found zero intact actors and zero
shrub mesh instances within 100 uu of each of the three coordinates above. That probe did not record a pre-break guest
baseline, so absence alone is not a complete destruction regression proof.

Both isolated executable hashes were rechecked against the supported build. Only `D:/PTLanes/vehicle-proxy` test junctions
were pointed at the development checkout for these runs. The user's `D:/PTLanes/a` pair remains on the pinned snapshot

### Further measurements and final matching changes

- Legacy vehicle control (`nocarmotion`), `20261105-110048-vehicle-proxy-carsync-nocarmotion`: 37/38; camera deviation
  47.3 uu against the previous car frame failed the 40 uu limit. Maximum motion step was 116 uu, with zero stalls.
  These are separate physical drives, not deterministic identical host trajectories; the speed-delta numbers alone
  cannot establish a smoothness improvement.
- Rendered 150 ms / 2% loss, `20261105-110512-vehicle-proxy-tunnel-carsync`: 44/44. Maximum sampled step 85.4 uu,
  zero stalls, camera deviation 25.2 uu. The runner retried a pre-mod host bootstrap stall before this gameplay run.
- Real driving breaks in the legacy-control run produced matching guest `PT|foliage applied actor` and
  `PT|foliage applied instance` logs. The rendered run also applied actor and instance removals. This establishes
  both real target representations, beyond the earlier absence-only probe.
- The rendered instance search took about five seconds for one target. The final implementation queries
  `GetInstancesOverlappingSphere` and checks only candidate transforms instead of scanning every instance.
  Returned integer wrappers are copied with `:get()` immediately. Actors/components are restricted to the current world.
  Original source positions match within 5 uu, so a nearby different shrub is not chosen by the old 100 uu tolerance.
- `20261105-111010-vehicle-proxy-foliage` is **discarded as positive evidence**: its automatic summary says 20/20,
  but a manual `RemoveInstance` probe removed the target after the wrapper bug prevented normal application.
- Corrected spatial-query run `20261105-111202-vehicle-proxy-foliage`: 20/20, no manual intervention. A host break at
  11:11:54.9638702 was applied on the guest at 11:11:54.9902183 among 1024 unrelated instances, approximately 26 ms later.
- Event-only negative control `20261105-111427-vehicle-proxy-foliage-nofoliageedge`: 18/20. Exactly the two removal
  checks failed, with no crash or PT error. Disabling the event observer leaves the polling fallback unable to catch
  the actor destroyed in the same callback.

The final foliage regression also places a second shrub only 25 uu from the target and checks that this neighbor remains.
The vehicle clock offset now slews by at most 0.5 ms per accepted sample to accommodate clock drift without abrupt changes.
`carsync` motion variants additionally require the receiver's host-timed-stream activation record, preventing a silent
fallback from masquerading as a new-stream pass.

## Final candidate status

Final foliage regression `20261105-111632-vehicle-proxy-foliage`: 20/20, including the neighbor at 25 uu and 1024 distant
instances. No manual intervention. The original target was removed and its neighbor remained.

Vehicle smoothness remains **unresolved**. The rendered attempt around 11:19 failed follow/camera/frame checks with a
6.583-second measurement gap; it was interrupted before a result archive was produced. Surviving shipping logs are copied
to `local/live-report-20261105-1119/`. Its cause has not been established.

Final headless 150 ms / 2% loss repeat `20261105-164729-vehicle-proxy-tunnel-carsync`: **43/45**, failing
`guest_car_follows` and `guest_camera_rides`. Host-timed motion was received; 699 guest samples, zero stalls, maximum
step 244.7 uu and maximum sample gap 462 ms. This disproves a claim that the new stream completely fixes guest jerks.
Earlier passing runs do not supersede these failures. No test threshold was relaxed. Further work needs to distinguish
engine movement/camera overwrites from frame stalls and test recovery across those stalls. Full-suite, two-PC Steam,
long-drive and release gates have not been completed; this is an experimental local checkpoint, not a certified preview.
